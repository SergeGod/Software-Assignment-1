For each expense the payer gets `+amount_cents` and each person in `shares` gets `-share`. For each payment the payer gets `+amount` and the receiver `-amount`. Positive means the group owes you, negative means you owe, and the total is always 0. `settle_up()` puts debtors and creditors in two heaps (amounts stored negated because `heapq` is a min-heap), pops the biggest of each, transfers `min(debt, credit)`, and pushes back whoever still has something left. Example: Alice owes Bob 10 and Bob owes Carol 10 gives one transfer, Alice to Carol. `service.py` gets the data from `ledger.get_group_ledger()` (never the ledger tables) and adds the rows from its own `payments` table; `record_payment()` only allows paying another member of the group. |
